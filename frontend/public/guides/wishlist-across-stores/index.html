BlindList

A wishlist that works across every store

Most wishlists live inside one store. That is fine until your list needs a book from one site, a kettle from another, and something from a small shop that has no wishlist feature at all.

The problem with per-store lists

  • People have to open several lists, and some need an account to view them.
  • Claimed items only update inside that one store.
  • Items from small shops and handmade sellers usually can't be added.

What to look for in a store-agnostic list

  • Paste any link. Any product page should work, from any retailer.
  • Names, notes and prices. Sizes, colours and a price range save buyers from guessing.
  • Categories. Group items so people can find something that fits their budget.
  • Purchase tracking. Buyers need to mark what they've bought so there are no duplicates.
  • No account for buyers. The easier it is to open, the more likely people are to use it.

Tips for a good list

  • Add the exact link so buyers get the right model or size.
  • Include a few cheap items and a few big ones.
  • Put non-product wishes in the notes, for example a favourite restaurant or experience.

BlindList works this way: add any item with a link, group it by category, and share one link with everyone. Items can come from any store.
