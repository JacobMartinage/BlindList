Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026.

What we store

  • Lists you create: the list name, items, notes, links, categories and prices.
  • Which items buyers have marked as purchased. The person who owns a list does not see this.
  • If you choose to give us an email address: that address, so we can send your list links and verify it.

Who we share it with

  • Hosting and database providers that run the service (Cloudflare and Supabase).
  • Resend, which delivers the emails we send.
  • Google Analytics, which gives us anonymous usage statistics such as page views. We do not send list names or item details to it.

We do not sell your data.

Amazon links

When a list is viewed by someone buying, we add our Amazon Associates tag to Amazon links that don't already have one. If you buy through such a link we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. The list owner always sees their original links. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.

Deleting your data

You can delete items from a list at any time. To have a list or an email address removed entirely, contact us through the site.
